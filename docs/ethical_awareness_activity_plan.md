# Ethical Awareness Activity Plan

## Purpose
The awareness activity is designed to measure whether participants can identify phishing indicators before and after a short training activity.

## Safe Method
Use mock screenshots, sample URLs, or short text examples. Do not send real phishing emails. Do not collect real passwords or sensitive data.

## Suggested Activity
1. Give participants 8 to 10 safe examples.
2. Ask them to classify each example as phishing or legitimate.
3. Provide a 10-minute awareness explanation covering:
   - suspicious URLs
   - urgent language
   - unknown senders
   - password requests
   - mismatched domains
   - unsafe attachments
4. Give another 8 to 10 examples.
5. Compare pre-training and post-training scores.

## Data to Collect
Only collect anonymous, non-sensitive data:
- participant number, such as P01, P02
- number of correct answers before training
- number of correct answers after training
- optional confidence score from 1 to 5

## Data Not to Collect
- names
- passwords
- real email addresses
- student IDs
- private messages
- financial information
- real login details

## Consent Statement
Participants should be informed that the activity is for project research, uses safe simulated examples, and does not involve real phishing or credential collection.
