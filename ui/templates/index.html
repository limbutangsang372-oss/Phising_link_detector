Ethics

Safe Research Scope

The project uses public or demonstration datasets and does not collect passwords. Any awareness-training results should be presented as simulated unless ethics approval and consent were obtained.

  1. Use public labelled datasets or approved lab data.
  2. Do not target real users with phishing messages.
  3. Anonymise any participant response data.
  4. Report limitations clearly in the dissertation.
